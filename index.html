Welcome to Vidhyatri

Education with purpose, values with vision.

Since 2005, Vidhyatri Public Higher Secondary School has served families in Betul. Our school supports students from their foundational years through higher secondary education, with learning opportunities in both English and Hindi.

We believe meaningful education brings academics, creativity, sports and personal development together.
