WELCOME TO VIDHYATRI

Where every learner finds their way to shine.

Located in Gouthana, Betul, Vidhyatri Public Higher Secondary School provides a learning journey from the early years through Class XII. We value academic progress, curiosity, kindness and confidence.

Our learning environment encourages students to participate in academics, sports and cultural activities—helping them build skills for school and beyond.
