GET TO KNOW US

Rooted in values. Growing with ambition.

Established in 2005, Vidhyatri Public Higher Secondary School has been part of the educational landscape of Gouthana, Betul. The school offers learning from kindergarten through Class XII, with English and Hindi as mediums of instruction.

Our educational approach recognises that every child learns differently. Along with classroom learning and regular evaluation, participation in sports, arts and cultural activities helps students develop confidence, curiosity and teamwork.

WHAT GUIDES US

A purpose that reaches beyond the classroom.

Our vision and mission statements below express the educational approach presented on this website; school leadership can refine them into approved official statements.

01 / OUR VISION

Confidence for tomorrow

To nurture curious, compassionate and capable learners who approach tomorrow with confidence, responsibility and a lifelong love of learning.

02 / OUR MISSION

Opportunity for every learner

To support academic foundations, encourage participation in diverse activities and create meaningful opportunities for personal development.

03 / OUR COMMITMENT

Character with knowledge

To promote care, respect, collaboration and consistent progress as important parts of every student's educational journey.
